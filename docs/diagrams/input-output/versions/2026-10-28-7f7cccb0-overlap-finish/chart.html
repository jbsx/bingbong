Important details behind this simplified picture
  • These boxes explain behavior, not literal enum states. The public runner exposes only idle and running.
  • Output happens throughout execution: page changes, status updates, answer cards and sometimes speech. A command's done event does not necessarily finish the whole Task.
  • A Task stops after five actions, two consecutive unchanged/unhelpful hops, an unrecoverable wall, an unavailable decision, or cancellation. Recoverable dialogs and dead-end menus have bounded recovery paths.
  • The Card Budget (#406, ADR 0088): every Jev ask, to every model, is built to fit 16,384 of the host’s tokens. What makes the hop is never cut — your words, the refs and text in view, the Verdict and passage, the verbs and the facts. What is further down yields first, in order: the right-clicks, then the links to open, then the shows, each list keeping what is nearest the view (#407): a block below holds what a link only leads to. The shows take the room the Choice’s 255 options leave in the same order, and every block below the view is offered — the budget, not a count of characters, is their bound. A card the host still refuses ends the hop unavailable as before; the record keeps the status and the host’s message.
  • The Answer Passage (#401, ADR 0087): every Jev ask that carries a Verdict also asks which block of the text in view states what was asked, or none. When the Verdict is a sure “yes” and the pick is sure, the Task ends by showing the page title over that block and speaking the block word for word, in place of “Done.” or silence; a Step with Steps to come speaks it too. No line is marked a question, and no model writes the words.
  • The Answer Passage by section (#482): the pick is read by section, not by block — a heading and the blocks under it to the next heading, or before any heading a run of table rows, list items or code — and a section is sure when its blocks’ probabilities summed reach 0.7 (the card still offers one block an option, as measured). The section is said whole: each block read off the page by its node, as a capture reads one, never the card’s 300 characters; a block gone from the page is said as the card held it. Text further down is never part of it.
  • Otherwise a Task’s end speaks one fixed line when there is a voice: “Done.” after more than one action, and “I couldn’t finish that.” at the five-action limit or when Jev stops answering after the first action. A single action that succeeds stays silent; a wall or an unsure end already says its own line. A Step with more Steps to come does not say “Done.”
  • What the app knows (#402): every action card but the Where Ask offers one option per fact the app holds — the time and date, today’s weather in the settings city, the song playing (the Player’s, or else the media on the page) and the timer — each line carrying the fact itself. The weather line says that another place or a website is a search, not this. When Jev picks one, the app fills a fixed sentence from what it holds as it fires, shows it as the answer and speaks it when there is a voice. No page action; the Idle Screen stays up; the Task ends there. A spoken partial read as a fact never fires early: “what’s the weather” may yet become “… in Gurgaon”. The weather is the Idle Screen’s own report, now fetched whether or not the Idle Screen shows and handed to the app’s main process.
  • A copy to the Shelf (#466, ADR 0095): every action card but the Where Ask and the screenshot ask offers a Capture Op by kind, last on the card (measured) — capture-N on each link (its address) and each image drawn in view at 48 px or more that no cue took as a control (its own bytes, or a screenshot of its box when the page cannot fetch them; such an image is a ref for this alone, numbered after every control), capture-view-N on each block the Text in view line quotes, capture-text-N on each block off the view, and capture-page (the title over the address) on a real page. The grammar has capture <ref> and capture page. One evaluate reads it; it goes to a file in bingbong_downloads (capture-<yyyymmdd-hhmmss>-<slug>) and on the clipboard, always both (an image of a type the clipboard cannot decode as the PNG the page draws of it), and “Copied the link to the Shelf.” (text, image, page) is shown and said. Read by Jev, the Task goes on: the next hop’s “Done so far” names the copy, the Verdict ends it, and what was copied leaves the card for the rest of the Task. The copies yield to the Card Budget after the shows and the links, before the right-clicks. Blocks the view has passed are offered too, under “Text further up the page”, numbered in page order before the blocks further down, and yield after them.
  • The app itself on the card (#420, ADR 0091): every action card but the Where Ask also offers the app’s own surfaces, each line carrying its state now — the activity panel (open, close, float over the page, dock beside it, its width, by the amount Choice) and the views (the browser, the Weight page, Settings and each of its sections, and, whenever the Idle Screen is not up, the Idle Screen itself over any of them, #458) — and one fixed “settings” line naming the settings. The settings are not each on the card (measured on the gate against every setting’s ops on every card). When Jev picks “settings” on the first ask, a second ask, the settings ask, is made over the same state (your words alone when that state and its card would pass the Card Budget): every setting’s ops by its kind with its state now — a switch on or off, one option per value of a choice or voice, a number moved by the amount Choice, a place or a time of day the words carry — the Slot, the amount, and none. A none or no answer there is no command and does not go to the Where Ask. At a later hop the “settings” line reads as no command. When Jev picks an op, the app changes the panel, the view (sent to the dashboard) or the settings — a spoken weather city is looked up first and the geocoder’s first match taken — then shows and speaks a fixed sentence naming the new value (“It already was.” when nothing moved). No page action; the Task ends there. A setting change keeps the Idle Screen up; a panel or view change wakes the dashboard. The Idle Screen’s op (#458) brings it up at once, over the turn still running, by the road the idle shortcut takes (the rest of that turn leaves it up), the pane, the Player and the Last Page kept as the idle timeout keeps them; its Answer is shown in the Feed and never spoken. There is no exact line for it: “go to the Idle Screen” is Jev’s, and so is the bare word idle. On a blank pane a view op still shows its view over the Idle Screen; only a panel op there stays under it. A number or place the words do not carry, or a place the geocoder does not find, fails with the failure line. A spoken partial read as an app op never fires early. A Command whose words ask for the panel may open it (ADR 0021 as amended).
  • A song that opens is not yet playing (#488): a play — and a queued song the Queue opens in the background — no longer ends “Playing “…”.” the moment the watch page holds a video. The Player reads the media element itself every 250 ms; it has started when it is not paused, not ended, holds data to play (readyState above 2) and its time has moved 1.5 s. Not playing by 2.5 s, it is pressed once with the element’s own play and read again for 2.5 s; still not playing, the watch page is opened again once in full and read and pressed once more the same way. Only a song that plays says “Playing “…”.” and ends done. Otherwise the Command ends not done, its Answer shown and said, with no Try line: ““<title>” opened but didn’t start.”. The Queue keeps the song, paused, so “play” or “resume” presses it, and its Remembered Song stays. During the wait the Capsule stays on the Loading stage (youtube.com, its seconds counting). Each try writes a media-start perf span, and a later line that ends the play (#489) ends the wait. Only when custody withholds the page is the start unread and taken as playing, as before.
  • Update by voice (#422): the update is an app op on the Appliance Card alone (beside the settings line, carrying the version running), never on the page card or the Where Ask. Picked surely, it asks for a newer Release (waiting at most 8 s), shows and says one fixed Answer and ends the line: “Installing version X, back in a moment.” when a Release is already downloaded, and once that Answer is said the app quits, installs and restarts (exit 75 under systemd) — unless a later line came first; “Downloading version X; it installs when the screen is idle.”; “Up to date, version X.”; “Updates only run in the installed app.” outside the AppImage; “Couldn’t check for updates.” when the check fails or does not answer in time. The page is untouched.
  • The Appliance Card (#485): a line the Local Match cannot settle is read first on a card built from what the appliance holds, with no page collect — one Jev ask (kind appliance). It offers the media element playing or started (the Player’s video, else the page’s own, read by one evaluate issued at speech start and waited at most 1 s at the Endpoint) with every Media Op its kind allows, fired on that element by kind; the Player’s Appliance Verbs (play, skip, …) with where beside play; the facts (time, weather, song, timer); the app’s ops (the panel, the views including the Idle Screen, the one “settings” line and its settings ask); resume on a blank pane; the Flag while there is a Task to flag; help; the Timer’s set and off; stop; and page and none; steps rides at hop 0. A pick fires and ends as its op ends — a Media Op by its own before/after read, a Player verb on the Player’s line, an app op on its fixed Answer — with no page collect, no later hop and never an Errand. The Flag pick keeps the last Task as the grammar’s Flag does (“Flagged.”). A play whose words point at the site open, or an op that cannot fire (a volume to no number, a timer with no length), goes on to the page card, where a play becomes the Site Scope search. Steps split there each read their hop 0 on the Appliance Card again. page or none goes on to the page card unchanged — the page collected, the Local Match over its refs, the one page-card ask, its none to the Where Ask and the Mark Ask, its not-done end to the Errand — and the Capsule says “Looking at <host>” (status thinking, word “Looking”). Its host failing: an Instant line (a grammar line a ref in hand carried as its label) fires as the Local Match reads it; any other goes on to the page card, whose failure is as before. A spoken line whose search, play or typed words could be a name skips the Appliance Card and waits for the speech-start page for its Name Match, as before. BINGBONG_APPLIANCE_CARD=off restores the road before #485 exactly; BINGBONG_APPLIANCE_ASKS=parallel (a measured arm, not the default) asks both cards at the Endpoint and drops the page card’s answer when the Appliance Card’s pick fires.
  • An exact line on the page in hand (#485): the Local Match’s line fires on its words before any page collect. Its label test (ADR 0089: a control whose label is the line makes it Jev’s) reads the page already in hand — the speech-start collect if it has settled, else the browser’s last snapshot while the pane is still on its address — and never waits; with no page in hand the line fires. The same holds for an Early Fire on a partial. A line a control in hand does carry as its label goes to the Appliance Card.
  • One reading on every page (#408, ADR 0089): every line but stop is read over the page before it fires (since #485, over the page in hand for an exact line, and the page card only after the Appliance Card). An exact line whose words a control on the page carries as its label (“next” beside a Next link, “continue” beside a Continue button) goes to Jev, and so do the lines that read intent (“not that one”, “show it”, “last page”, “where was I”) and “the first result”. open <one word> opens without an ask only a Known Address, a site the app has landed on, and opens that site; any other word is Jev’s, and so are the bare words settings, weight, dashboard, panel and feed: “open settings” goes to Jev, not to settings.com (#420). With a song queued the card offers correct, correct_back and forget; a volume or speed is one op whose direction and size the amount Choice gives. A landing ends the Task with its line, no one asked, only on a fact: the status the site served (404, 410, 5xx) or a challenge address; a title or text that reads as not found, unavailable, a wall or empty goes on to the next hop, even after a local open, whose Verdict reads it, and its line is said only when Jev does not answer. Over a pop-up Jev is asked, a cookie wall included, and its sure pick goes first; the cookie rule’s reject (never one that pays or subscribes), then its accept, are the fallback.
  • The pop-up’s own text (#416, ADR 0076): over a pop-up, Jev is offered each piece of its text in view that no control holds, beside its controls and Escape — a × or “Continue without” that nothing marks as a control is still a place a click lands. A piece Jev picks is clicked where it shows and is a rung of the Dismissal Ladder like a control, checked for a pop-up still open; one whose words go through the pop-up (sign in, subscribe, accept…) is never offered. Jev is told a dismissal never does what the pop-up asks, only leaves without doing it.
  • A pop-up that leaves (#417): a click on one of the pop-up’s own controls, or on a piece of its text, waits until no pop-up is open or it shows something else, at most two seconds, before the next hop reads the page — a cookie sheet that takes a moment to go is read gone, and a control that redraws the pop-up (Preferences, a switch, more of the wall) costs no wait. Whether the pop-up is still open is read from the page after every action on it, whatever the action’s own outcome: a control refused as hidden or inert, or one in a frame that went with the sheet, has not failed when the pop-up left, and the Task goes on from the page it leaves.
  • The Flag (#421): “mark this as a failure”, “mark as failure” (#478), “flag that” or “that was wrong”, optionally followed by “with the hint …”, is an exact line read before anything else. It never reaches Jev and is no Command: nothing is superseded, no page is touched, and a Task under way goes on. The app keeps the Task before it (a stop or help is no Task) — every hop and Step, by its Task id, its asks and a shot of the pane as it stands — in logs/flags/<taskId>/, out of the 7-day purge, writes a flag record to the decision log, and shows and says “Flagged.”; with no Task yet in this run, “Nothing to flag yet.” A spoken partial that may yet be a flag line — even its first word, “mark”, “flag” or “that” — never fires early. pnpm bugs:candidates lists flagged Tasks first, with their hints.
  • A Site Question (#423): when an action’s page raises its own yes/no dialog (a native confirm()), the page freezes on it and the Task that fired the action stays in flight — never superseded. The app says “The page asks: … Yes or no?” and your next line, spoken in the Hot Listen still open or typed, is read as its answer before anything else (before the Flag, the Local Match or Jev’s action card). “yes”, “ok”, “okay” and “confirm” answer OK; “no”, “cancel” and every Dismiss Phrase answer Cancel, with no Jev call. Any other line is one Jev ask (kind site) with three choices: OK, Cancel, or a new command. A sure OK or Cancel answers the page; a new command answers Cancel and then runs as an ordinary Command; Jev unsure (under the 0.7 Choice bar) or unavailable answers Cancel and says “Refused the page’s question.” An answer shows “Answered OK.” or “Answered Cancel.”, and the paused Task goes on from its action. If the page closes its question while Jev reads (it navigated away), the line runs as usual. While a question is open no partial fires early: the Endpoint’s line answers it. An alert() needs no answer: it is accepted and “The page says: …” is said. A leave-page warning met while a navigate, back, forward or reload Command leaves the page is accepted (Electron settles it); otherwise the page stays. Electron never opens prompt(), so there is no prompt answer.
  • Upload by voice (#424, ADR 0092): the pane intercepts every file chooser from the start, and re-arms that the moment its debugger detaches, so the system’s Open dialog never opens. When a click or press fires and the page opens its file chooser — a file field, or a site’s own button over a hidden one — the runner makes a second ask, the Shelf ask: which file on the Shelf your words mean, or none. The Shelf is the app’s downloads folder and the Settings upload folder, each read flat (no folders below, no hidden or still-downloading files), newest first, each file by its name, age and size, cut to the Card Budget; the field’s accepts and takes-several-files ride the ask for Jev to weigh. A file picked is set on the field (the page sees the change), nothing is submitted, and “Attached <name>.” is shown and said. Otherwise nothing is set and a failure line is shown and said: “No file on the shelf fits that upload.” for an empty Shelf or Jev’s none; “I couldn’t finish that.” with no model or no answer from Jev; “That didn’t work.” when setting the file fails. The upload record names the reason. Either way the Task ends there (ended terminal), so no later hop can send the form; sending it is a line of its own. A chooser the page opens outside a Command is left unanswered and dropped by the next click or key; a field left unanswered opens again on the next line. On the action card a file input is a file field with accepts "…" and takes several files on its line; no option is added. Settings gains the upload folder: on the settings ask it is set to the folder the words name — among the home folder’s folders and those one below, its whole path below home or else its own name among the words — or cleared; the Answer names the folder, and a folder not found fails with the failure line.
  • The Status Capsule’s stages (#428): the moment a line is taken, before the page is read, the Capsule says “Thinking…” with the heard words beside it; it covers the page read and every ask. Each hop then says “Acting…” with the action’s words (the Feed entry’s own), and “Loading…” from the action’s first visible effect (the page leaving, the page changing) through its navigation or settle, until the next hop reads the page, its seconds running on across that hand-over; a later hop’s “Thinking…” carries its hop number, and a Verdict hop that ends the Task only thinks. The seconds beside the stage are that stage’s own. Past its own bound in one stage the hint names the wait: “still thinking” or “still acting” past 1.7 s, “page still loading” past 7.2 s (#442: the dev PC’s p95 of the spans that hold each stage, still a guess until the new per-stage spans date it). “Loading…” is on the host it loads (#442), re-named when a click’s navigation lands elsewhere. The Idle Screen shows the Capsule for every working stage, from the first “Thinking…”; it still goes down only on a Command that touches the pane. A Flag shows no stage of its own, and the pill stays on the Task it leaves alone; Escape or a stop while a line is being read says “Stopped” at once. Between one hop’s end and the next hop’s stage the pill holds its stage (300 ms) rather than blinking “Ready”. The screen never names Jev or the Decision Model, and nothing is chimed or said while you wait.
  • The ear’s waits (#442): from launch until the models that hear are in, the ear is “Starting” — the Capsule, the Voice Prompt and the Idle Screen say “loading the ear” with its seconds and never invite speech (the empty Feed’s hint is blank), and the Capsule is quiet, never “Ready”. The run is ready only then, and the transcriber loads from there; a line taken before the transcriber is in is held, still “Starting”, with its words and the seconds since its Endpoint, which run on into “Transcribing”. Only a wake heard while the transcriber loaded hears “I’m ready” when it is in, and only if no line is being taken. While words are read, “Transcribing” carries them — the Utterance’s last partial, or “your words” — and its seconds; the words ellipsize, the seconds never do.
  • A wake that brings no words (#483): a wake listen runs the Hot Listen’s silence clock (hotListenSilenceMs, 8 s by default) from the moment it opens, not only once it has submitted a line. A blip (an empty transcript, or a capture too short to keep) restarts it; speech under way, a line playing and a Task still under way hold it, as they hold a Hot Listen’s. When it runs out with no line the ear closes, says and shows nothing new, and the wake monitor hears again; a listen-closed perf span (heard: false) records it, and pnpm perf:report counts these as false wakes. A hotkey listen is unchanged: no clock closes it.
  • A cold start that fails (#447): when the VAD, the wake word or the transcriber does not load, the wait ends — the Voice Prompt and the Idle Screen say “Ear did not start — type, or restart”, the Capsule “Ear did not start” (quiet, never “Ready”) with “type, or restart” beside it, the count gone and the orb grey, and the empty Feed’s hint stays blank. It is shown, not spoken, save to a wake heard while the transcriber loaded, which hears “The ear did not start. Type, or restart the app.” in place of “I’m ready”. A voice.ear.load record goes to the fault log. A later load of that model that succeeds clears the line to what is still loading, or to the rest hint. Typed lines run as ever. In the Feed panel, TRANSCRIBING, wider than the panel at 65 px, is set at 40 px there.
  • The narrated wait and the next line (#438): the Capsule’s pill says each stage in the Feed’s own words — “Thinking…”, then the action’s word (“Opening…”, “Searching…”, “Clicking…”, “Playing…”; “Acting…” for a verb with no word of its own), then “Loading…” — and the hint says what it is on in your words (the host, the search terms, the control’s label), never the raw line; the Feed’s Run Detail lines are the same words (“Opening example.com”, “Loading example.com”). A failure’s Answer and spoken line end with a line that would have worked, by the failure and the Command’s kind, fixed in code and never asked of Jev: “Try “press escape”.” over a pop-up, “Try “back”.” on a page that is no use, over a cookie notice or pop-up that stayed, and for an address that would not open, “Try “read”.” for an item the page lacks or an unsure end, “Try “navigate <your words>”.” for what was not found here; a song that would not play names none. Every Try line is one the Local Match reads with no Jev call (#445): “search …” and “not that one” are Jev’s, and “click accept” fires only beside a button that says it word for word. A control on the page labelled with the line’s very words (a Back link beside “back”) still makes it Jev’s. “No response” gives its reason in your words (“Those words aren’t a command.”, “Reading the words took too long.”) and a “Try” line; it never names Jev. In the Feed a Command keeps one time, its result’s.
  • The last result on screen (#436): with the Feed panel shut, once the line has ended, the Status Capsule keeps the sentence the app said aloud for it — a page read’s first part, a Task’s closing line, a page’s own question — beside “Ready”, until your next line takes the Capsule; your live words take its place while you speak. With the panel open the Feed carries it. The state word is 65 px, read from 2 m.
  • The Peek Card is the Ledger Strip (#475), the Turn Card’s fold (#474) in another frame: with the Feed panel shut, a band the pane’s width at its bottom (340 px) opens on the wake — the ear opening for a line — or on a typed line’s “Thinking”, and carries the whole journey: a rail of six stations (Heard, Thinking, Acting, Loading, Speaking, Done) lit to the live one, a station the turn skips unlit; the stage word at 65 px in the Capsule’s own words (“Listening…”, “Transcribing…”, “Thinking…”, the action’s word, “Loading…”, “Speaking…”, “Done”, “Failed”, “Stopped”, “No response”) with the waveform while the ear hears or the stage’s seconds counted from its own event, the ended line’s whole time in grey; two lines for the words of the moment (the live partial, the last words, the heard line, the action’s object, the host, the sentence, the Answer said plain); and five history rows, a row per step, clocked from the turn’s start. Its geometry is fixed: nothing moves between states. An ear that closes on nothing closes it; speech beginning, a new typed line or the next Command restarts it; the heard line stands before the Command’s rows wherever main reports it. A stage reads as the Capsule reads it (stageEntered): an action’s word and object ride into its own hop’s Loading only, and the same wait carried into the next hop’s turn keeps its seconds. The band keeps its end for 15 s after the line’s sentence has been spoken (#470), then goes; a tap opens the panel, and opening the panel or the next line ends it sooner; nothing pauses it. Over the Idle Screen the Turn Card reports the line instead (#474). This changes what you see, not what is run.
  • A line may contain multiple Steps. Each Step is read afresh and runs only after the previous Step succeeds.
  • The Hearing: before any Jev ask on a spoken line, the whole transcript is compared by sound with the names on the page and the names you taught the app, and the matches are written on the card beside your words. It only informs Jev; the spelling fix to the chosen Slot still happens afterwards. A typed line hears nothing. One road skips Jev (#478): a spoken line whose words are all one command phrase as heard, its first word the phrase’s own, is read as that exact line — a Flag head, or a Local Match that needs no page — before anything else reads it, as if the ear had heard it right (“Mark as junior.” flags, “Loop the song.” loops); what was heard rides the record. A line only partly covered, or whose first word was heard as another verb (“Stop this song.” is no “skip this song”), goes to Jev as heard.
  • The hearing receipt (#441): while listening, the said-line reads “say a command” until speech starts. On the audio frame where the app confirms speech (about 0.1 s into it, the same frame that starts the page read), the Voice Prompt, the Idle Screen’s Voice Line and the Status Capsule’s hint switch it to “…”, still under the word “Listening”. The first partial’s words replace the “…” after 0.5 s of speech. The “…” goes back to “say a command” when the capture is dropped (a blip, or the app’s own voice), and gives way to “Transcribing” at the Endpoint. This changes what you see, not what is run.
  • The Errand (#467, ADR 0095), on by default since #468 measured where the Planner enters (arm C, the hand-off, won as a tie inside the noise; BINGBONG_PLANNER=off is the one kill switch, no Settings line): the exact line errand <words> starts one with no Jev call, and a Task that ends at its limit, unsure or unavailable hands its line, its Steps’ “Done so far” and its last Answer over instead of saying “I couldn’t finish that.” / “I’m not sure this is it.” — with no Planner configured that end is said as before, and errand <words> says “The planner is not set up: its key is missing.” Each step a fresh card is built by the same builder as Jev’s, cut to Jev’s Card Budget, and goes with the memory to the Planner (GLM over Z.ai); the Capsule says “Errand” with the Goal Line while it thinks, and the Planner’s why while it acts or reads. It answers one of: an action (fired through the same executor as any Command, its Answer shown and nothing spoken; one not on the card is refused into the memory), read a part of the page’s text into the memory (never aloud), ask one question, or done. An action leaves the appliance when the Planner says leaves, or a click or Enter lands on a control whose label carries post, send, pay, buy, order, delete, submit, publish, reply, comment or (un)subscribe; it is said back (“This will post: <text>. Yes or no?”) by the Site Question’s road, and only an exact yes/ok/okay/confirm fires it; anything else, or no line in the Hot Listen window, refuses into the memory and the Errand goes on. A question’s answer is the next line; none in the window ends it: “<question> Ending there.” Done says the Planner’s own sentence, said and shown; a done or a question back is a Feed entry in the Planner’s words like any step, and an Errand ended after a refused confirm is recorded declined. An Enter leaves when any field typed into on that page carries a leaving word. The 90 s cap counts the work only (paused while you are asked): “Done so far: …. Ran out of time.”; a Planner call past 30 s or gone: “The planner did not answer. Done so far: …”; a reply that is none of the answers goes back once, and a second in a row ends it so. A later line, Escape or the Abort Head ends it as a Task, saying nothing. While a confirm or question waits nothing fires early and the run is not idle, so a Hot Listen stays open. Both 90 s and 30 s are guesses.
  • The one ask (#477, ADR 0076 as amended): Jev is never asked on a partial transcript. An Early Fire is a Local Match line alone (“scroll down”, “go back”, a key), under the holds it had; every other partial waits for its Endpoint, where the line is read once over the page prepared at speech start. On the kiosk every Early Fire on a Jev-read partial was wrong (6 of 6, a median lead of 161 ms). An ask that a host refuses (a 429 or another status), lets time out, or fails to carry goes at once, with the same bytes, to the next Fallback Host BINGBONG_DECISION_FALLBACKS names (none by default); every attempt but the last is bounded at 2,250 ms; “No response” is said only when every host failed, and the screen shows nothing of the hosts — the wait stays “Thinking” with its seconds. The grammar reads a spoken line again without the ear’s trailing punctuation (“Help.” is help); the Mark Ask’s image is scaled to the host’s 1,568 px long edge.
  • A page scroll ends on a cheap check (#484): one read of where the page stands before the wheel and one after it, never a page collect, so the line ends as soon as the move is seen. A check that finds nothing moved says “End of page”; where the page had room to move, it first lets the wheel settle and reads again. A Scroll Op on a region and a Show Op end on their own before and after reads, and a key press leaves the page to be read anew. The page's collect after these actions waits for the next line that needs the page; nothing showed the old delta, and the next hop, the next Errand step and the next line's reading each collect the page anyway. A collect gives up waiting for its controls' states (pressed, expanded, checked) after 1 s and goes on without them.
  • The Overlap (#489): a line given while another still runs no longer ends it on arrival. While a line runs, the Appliance Card carries it — its words on a labelled line, how long before the new line it was given, its stage in the Status Capsule’s words, its Task’s actions so far and the media a play is opening — offers the fact say_doing (“Still on “<words>”: <stage>.”, or for a song a play is opening “Still opening “<song>”: loading youtube.com.”) and asks one more question, its Fate: ends, joins, waits or goes on. A Fate below 0.7, or a failed host, is goes on. The runner acts on it before the new line fires: ends cancels the running line’s action and ends its Task or Errand (an ended play stops the pane’s load and pauses it, and its song is never pressed to start after, unless the line that ended it takes the pane), its grey Answer and its spoken line “Stopped <its Acting stage>.”, said ahead of the new line’s own sentence (alone when that line says none); joins ends it unsaid (“Stopped: a later Command came first.”, grey) and reads both lines’ words in order as one line on one more ask, which offers no Steps: the joined line takes one step, and a song it plays is said (“Playing “…”.”), not only shown; waits shows the Waiting stage naming what it waits for, holds the line until the running one ends however it ends, then reads it again against what is there; goes on fires the new line beside the running one, which keeps its own end. An exact line still fires on its words with no ask, its Fate by kind: one that takes the pane (navigate, search, back, forward, reload, resume, an errand line, a play that opens now) ends the running line, stop ends it, a control on media a play is still opening waits (and never fires early), any other goes on; an Early Fire follows the same rule. A Jev-read Command that takes the pane ends the running line unless its Fate is joins or waits. A line with nothing running sees the card byte for byte as before. stop, “never mind” and an abort (Escape, the Abort Head) now say “Stopped.” aloud. With BINGBONG_APPLIANCE_CARD=off the old road stands: a line’s arrival supersedes the one running, which shows “Stopped: a later Command came first.” and says nothing.
  • Escape, abort, “stop” or a later line whose Fate ends it can invalidate earlier work. The charts omit those arrows from every box to keep the main paths readable; the Overlap is drawn once, at the foot of the whole journey.
