Important details behind this simplified picture
  • These boxes explain behavior, not literal enum states. The public runner exposes only idle and running.
  • Output happens throughout execution: page changes, status updates, answer cards and sometimes speech. A command's done event does not necessarily finish the whole Task.
  • A Task stops after five actions, two consecutive unchanged/unhelpful hops, an unrecoverable wall, an unavailable decision, or cancellation. Recoverable dialogs and dead-end menus have bounded recovery paths.
  • The Card Budget (#406, ADR 0088): every Jev ask, to every model, is built to fit 16,384 of the host’s tokens. What makes the hop is never cut — your words, the refs and text in view, the Verdict and passage, the verbs and the facts. What is further down yields first, in order: the right-clicks, then the links to open, then the shows, each list keeping what is nearest the view (#407): a block below holds what a link only leads to. The shows take the room the Choice’s 255 options leave in the same order, and every block below the view is offered — the budget, not a count of characters, is their bound. A card the host still refuses ends the hop unavailable as before; the record keeps the status and the host’s message.
  • The Answer Passage (#401, ADR 0087): every Jev ask that carries a Verdict also asks which block of the text in view states what was asked, or none. When the Verdict is a sure “yes” and the pick is sure, the Task ends by showing the page title over that block and speaking the block word for word, in place of “Done.” or silence; a Step with Steps to come speaks it too. No line is marked a question, and no model writes the words.
  • Otherwise a Task’s end speaks one fixed line when there is a voice: “Done.” after more than one action, and “I couldn’t finish that.” at the five-action limit or when Jev stops answering after the first action. A single action that succeeds stays silent; a wall or an unsure end already says its own line. A Step with more Steps to come does not say “Done.”
  • What the app knows (#402): every action card but the Where Ask offers one option per fact the app holds — the time and date, today’s weather in the settings city, the song playing (the Player’s, or else the media on the page) and the timer — each line carrying the fact itself. The weather line says that another place or a website is a search, not this. When Jev picks one, the app fills a fixed sentence from what it holds as it fires, shows it as the answer and speaks it when there is a voice. No page action; the Idle Screen stays up; the Task ends there. A spoken partial read as a fact never fires early: “what’s the weather” may yet become “… in Gurgaon”. The weather is the Idle Screen’s own report, now fetched whether or not the Idle Screen shows and handed to the app’s main process.
  • One reading on every page (#408, ADR 0089): every line but stop is read over the page before it fires. An exact line whose words a control on the page carries as its label (“next” beside a Next link, “continue” beside a Continue button) goes to Jev, and so do the lines that read intent (“not that one”, “show it”, “last page”, “where was I”) and “the first result”. open <one word> opens without an ask only a Known Address, a site the app has landed on, and opens that site; any other word is Jev’s. With a song queued the card offers correct, correct_back and forget; a volume or speed is one op whose direction and size the amount Choice gives. A landing ends the Task with its line, no one asked, only on a fact: the status the site served (404, 410, 5xx) or a challenge address; a title or text that reads as not found, unavailable, a wall or empty goes on to the next hop, even after a local open, whose Verdict reads it, and its line is said only when Jev does not answer. Over a pop-up Jev is asked, a cookie wall included, and its sure pick goes first; the cookie rule’s reject (never one that pays or subscribes), then its accept, are the fallback.
  • The pop-up’s own text (#416, ADR 0076): over a pop-up, Jev is offered each piece of its text in view that no control holds, beside its controls and Escape — a × or “Continue without” that nothing marks as a control is still a place a click lands. A piece Jev picks is clicked where it shows and is a rung of the Dismissal Ladder like a control, checked for a pop-up still open; one whose words go through the pop-up (sign in, subscribe, accept…) is never offered. Jev is told a dismissal never does what the pop-up asks, only leaves without doing it.
  • A line may contain multiple Steps. Each Step is read afresh and runs only after the previous Step succeeds.
  • The Hearing: before any Jev ask on a spoken line, the whole transcript is compared by sound with the names on the page and the names you taught the app, and the matches are written on the card beside your words. It only informs Jev; the spelling fix to the chosen Slot still happens afterwards. A typed line hears nothing.
  • Escape, abort or newer input can invalidate earlier work. The charts omit those arrows from every box to keep the main paths readable.
