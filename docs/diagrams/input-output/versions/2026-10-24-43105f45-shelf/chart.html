Important details behind this simplified picture
  • These boxes explain behavior, not literal enum states. The public runner exposes only idle and running.
  • Output happens throughout execution: page changes, status updates, answer cards and sometimes speech. A command's done event does not necessarily finish the whole Task.
  • A Task stops after five actions, two consecutive unchanged/unhelpful hops, an unrecoverable wall, an unavailable decision, or cancellation. Recoverable dialogs and dead-end menus have bounded recovery paths.
  • The Card Budget (#406, ADR 0088): every Jev ask, to every model, is built to fit 16,384 of the host’s tokens. What makes the hop is never cut — your words, the refs and text in view, the Verdict and passage, the verbs and the facts. What is further down yields first, in order: the right-clicks, then the links to open, then the shows, each list keeping what is nearest the view (#407): a block below holds what a link only leads to. The shows take the room the Choice’s 255 options leave in the same order, and every block below the view is offered — the budget, not a count of characters, is their bound. A card the host still refuses ends the hop unavailable as before; the record keeps the status and the host’s message.
  • The Answer Passage (#401, ADR 0087): every Jev ask that carries a Verdict also asks which block of the text in view states what was asked, or none. When the Verdict is a sure “yes” and the pick is sure, the Task ends by showing the page title over that block and speaking the block word for word, in place of “Done.” or silence; a Step with Steps to come speaks it too. No line is marked a question, and no model writes the words.
  • Otherwise a Task’s end speaks one fixed line when there is a voice: “Done.” after more than one action, and “I couldn’t finish that.” at the five-action limit or when Jev stops answering after the first action. A single action that succeeds stays silent; a wall or an unsure end already says its own line. A Step with more Steps to come does not say “Done.”
  • What the app knows (#402): every action card but the Where Ask offers one option per fact the app holds — the time and date, today’s weather in the settings city, the song playing (the Player’s, or else the media on the page) and the timer — each line carrying the fact itself. The weather line says that another place or a website is a search, not this. When Jev picks one, the app fills a fixed sentence from what it holds as it fires, shows it as the answer and speaks it when there is a voice. No page action; the Idle Screen stays up; the Task ends there. A spoken partial read as a fact never fires early: “what’s the weather” may yet become “… in Gurgaon”. The weather is the Idle Screen’s own report, now fetched whether or not the Idle Screen shows and handed to the app’s main process.
  • The app itself on the card (#420, ADR 0091): every action card but the Where Ask also offers the app’s own surfaces, each line carrying its state now — the activity panel (open, close, float over the page, dock beside it, its width, by the amount Choice) and the views (the browser, the Weight page, Settings and each of its sections) — and one fixed “settings” line naming the settings. The settings are not each on the card (measured on the gate against every setting’s ops on every card). When Jev picks “settings” on the first ask, a second ask, the settings ask, is made over the same state (your words alone when that state and its card would pass the Card Budget): every setting’s ops by its kind with its state now — a switch on or off, one option per value of a choice or voice, a number moved by the amount Choice, a place or a time of day the words carry — the Slot, the amount, and none. A none or no answer there is no command and does not go to the Where Ask. At a later hop the “settings” line reads as no command. When Jev picks an op, the app changes the panel, the view (sent to the dashboard) or the settings — a spoken weather city is looked up first and the geocoder’s first match taken — then shows and speaks a fixed sentence naming the new value (“It already was.” when nothing moved). No page action; the Task ends there. A setting change keeps the Idle Screen up; a panel or view change wakes the dashboard. On a blank pane a view op still shows its view over the Idle Screen; only a panel op there stays under it. A number or place the words do not carry, or a place the geocoder does not find, fails with the failure line. A spoken partial read as an app op never fires early. A Command whose words ask for the panel may open it (ADR 0021 as amended).
  • One reading on every page (#408, ADR 0089): every line but stop is read over the page before it fires. An exact line whose words a control on the page carries as its label (“next” beside a Next link, “continue” beside a Continue button) goes to Jev, and so do the lines that read intent (“not that one”, “show it”, “last page”, “where was I”) and “the first result”. open <one word> opens without an ask only a Known Address, a site the app has landed on, and opens that site; any other word is Jev’s, and so are the bare words settings, weight, dashboard, panel and feed: “open settings” goes to Jev, not to settings.com (#420). With a song queued the card offers correct, correct_back and forget; a volume or speed is one op whose direction and size the amount Choice gives. A landing ends the Task with its line, no one asked, only on a fact: the status the site served (404, 410, 5xx) or a challenge address; a title or text that reads as not found, unavailable, a wall or empty goes on to the next hop, even after a local open, whose Verdict reads it, and its line is said only when Jev does not answer. Over a pop-up Jev is asked, a cookie wall included, and its sure pick goes first; the cookie rule’s reject (never one that pays or subscribes), then its accept, are the fallback.
  • The pop-up’s own text (#416, ADR 0076): over a pop-up, Jev is offered each piece of its text in view that no control holds, beside its controls and Escape — a × or “Continue without” that nothing marks as a control is still a place a click lands. A piece Jev picks is clicked where it shows and is a rung of the Dismissal Ladder like a control, checked for a pop-up still open; one whose words go through the pop-up (sign in, subscribe, accept…) is never offered. Jev is told a dismissal never does what the pop-up asks, only leaves without doing it.
  • A pop-up that leaves (#417): a click on one of the pop-up’s own controls, or on a piece of its text, waits until no pop-up is open or it shows something else, at most two seconds, before the next hop reads the page — a cookie sheet that takes a moment to go is read gone, and a control that redraws the pop-up (Preferences, a switch, more of the wall) costs no wait. Whether the pop-up is still open is read from the page after every action on it, whatever the action’s own outcome: a control refused as hidden or inert, or one in a frame that went with the sheet, has not failed when the pop-up left, and the Task goes on from the page it leaves.
  • The Flag (#421): “mark this as a failure”, “flag that” or “that was wrong”, optionally followed by “with the hint …”, is an exact line read before anything else. It never reaches Jev and is no Command: nothing is superseded, no page is touched, and a Task under way goes on. The app keeps the Task before it (a stop or help is no Task) — every hop and Step, by its Task id, its asks and a shot of the pane as it stands — in logs/flags/<taskId>/, out of the 7-day purge, writes a flag record to the decision log, and shows and says “Flagged.”; with no Task yet in this run, “Nothing to flag yet.” A spoken partial that may yet be a flag line — even its first word, “mark”, “flag” or “that” — never fires early. pnpm bugs:candidates lists flagged Tasks first, with their hints.
  • Upload by voice (#424, ADR 0092): the pane intercepts every file chooser from the start, so the system’s Open dialog never opens. When a click or press fires and the page opens its file chooser — a file field, or a site’s own button over a hidden one — the runner makes a second ask, the Shelf ask: which file on the Shelf your words mean, or none. The Shelf is the app’s downloads folder and the Settings upload folder, each read flat (no folders below, no hidden or still-downloading files), newest first, each file by its name, age and size, cut to the Card Budget; the field’s accepts and takes-several-files ride the ask for Jev to weigh. A file picked is set on the field (the page’s change fires), nothing is submitted, and “Attached <name>.” is shown and said. An empty Shelf, no model configured, no answer, none, or a set that fails sets nothing and shows and says “No file on the shelf fits that upload.” in the failure colour. Either way the Task ends there (ended terminal), so no later hop can send the form; sending it is a line of its own. A chooser the page opens outside a Command is left unanswered and dropped by the next click or key. On the action card a file input is a file field with accepts "…" and takes several files on its line; no option is added. Settings gains the upload folder: on the settings ask it is set to the folder the words name — looked up among the home folder’s folders and those one below — or cleared; the Answer names the folder, and a folder not found fails with the failure line.
  • A line may contain multiple Steps. Each Step is read afresh and runs only after the previous Step succeeds.
  • The Hearing: before any Jev ask on a spoken line, the whole transcript is compared by sound with the names on the page and the names you taught the app, and the matches are written on the card beside your words. It only informs Jev; the spelling fix to the chosen Slot still happens afterwards. A typed line hears nothing.
  • Escape, abort or newer input can invalidate earlier work. The charts omit those arrows from every box to keep the main paths readable.
